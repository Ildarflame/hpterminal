HP Terminal

Trade Hyperliquid perps from your phone.

HP Terminal is a mobile-first trading terminal for Hyperliquid. Crypto and stock perps in one place, one ticket that fits your thumb, and the fee shown before every order.

  • Your account stays yours. Orders go to your own Hyperliquid account, and HP Terminal never holds your funds.
  • Crypto and US stock markets in one list, each with its logo, live price and one market per asset.
  • Three looks for three ways to trade: Column, Cockpit and Hold.
